## Why this fresh suite is different

v0.3.1 successfully repaired F8–F11 on exposed regressions by introducing an external trust policy. v0.4 therefore does not replay those local provenance attacks. It asks whether the **trust policy itself** has an independently authenticated root.

The threat model is deliberately narrow: policy configurability is not treated as a bug. A failure occurs only when a downstream caller can construct or substitute a policy and thereby change the protected training/evaluation identity of benchmark-derived content without any authority above that policy.

## Interpretation

The current design has moved the trust boundary outward, but one level remains unresolved: `load_trust_policy()` authenticates policy **contents referenced by the policy**, not the identity of the policy root itself. A caller who controls the policy argument can still redefine which source/suite/family is authoritative. In addition, the policy builder does not impose a global case identity namespace, and a suite blob is not canonically bound to one family-root/manifest set.

This is a structural trust-root problem, not a single-case scoring issue. No case-specific hardcoding should be used to repair it.

## Required next repair

A v0.4.1 repair should introduce a higher-level immutable/canonical policy-root registry or equivalent authenticated root that:

1. pins allowed trust-policy path + blob/signature/root ID independently of the caller;
2. rejects arbitrary/off-repository policy substitution for protected export flows;
3. records a global benchmark case identity namespace or collision policy across trusted suites;
4. binds suite identity to canonical suite path + family-root + manifest membership, preventing same-blob alternate-root replay;
5. preserves existing source/manifest/payload mismatch fail-closed behavior.

After repair, this v0.4 suite becomes exposed regression evidence only. A later independent fresh suite must be created after the repaired implementation is frozen.
