## Structural changes

1. `materialize_s5_cases.py` establishes the S5 case boundary by joining raw case + family manifest + suite contract. It records family/suite/split, source paths, SHA-256 hashes and training eligibility.
2. `export_training_data.py` rejects approved candidates from `heldout` and `regression` partitions and propagates provenance for eligible dev exports.
3. `clinical-case.schema.json` now requires the diagnostic decision contract (`graph_eval` plus required nodes, edges and reasoning path) and defines typed exemption/provenance structures.
4. `s5_release_gate.py` requires explicit `gold_approved` status for every family and fails closed otherwise.
5. Historical S5 v0.1 first-observation artifacts are protected by blob SHA rather than being recomputed against the repaired implementation.

## Interpretation

S5 is now structurally capable of maintaining benchmark/training boundaries, but it is **not released**. The existing P0 `heldout` cases were visible before this repair and can never become fresh evidence. The next valid proof is to freeze v0.1.1 and then create a new independent family/suite for first-use fresh evaluation of materialization, leakage/shortcut resistance, decision-node coverage and verifier alignment.
