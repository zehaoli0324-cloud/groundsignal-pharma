# S5 v0.8 — Genuinely Fresh Post-calibration Lineage Evaluation

Status: **FRESH STRUCTURAL FAIL / RELEASE BLOCKED**

## 术语表

- **Lineage（数据血缘）**：训练候选是否由受保护 benchmark 内容改写、翻译、裁剪或拼接而来。
- **Fresh evaluation（全新评测）**：目标实现先冻结，之后才创建测试；首次结果不可因修复而覆盖。
- **Semantic abstraction（语义抽象）**：用别名、同义词、上位概念或数值改写保留原决策逻辑。
- **Mosaic contamination（拼接污染）**：把多个受保护案例的片段或推理约束组合成一个新候选。
- **Fail-closed（失败即关闭）**：不能确认安全时拒绝进入训练，而不是默认放行。

## 4. Diagnosis

F28 shows that the protected-exclusive lexical anchors do not establish semantic equivalence across
languages. The candidate retained the protected decision contract but scored only 0.485912 record
similarity and was allowed.

F31 shows a boundary mismatch as well as a representation limit. The detector recognized elevated
risk and returned `REVIEW`, but policy construction and exporter validation reject only `BLOCK`.
Consequently a known protected-source mosaic remained eligible to cross the automated boundary.

The English clean control also returned `REVIEW`. It was not blocked from export, so this is not a
false block. It is nevertheless retained as a failed strict control because the protocol required
unambiguous `ALLOW`; one of two clean controls entering review also warns that simply converting all
`REVIEW` decisions to hard blocks would create collateral damage.

F29 and F30 demonstrate useful generalization: the detector blocked both unseen transformation
families without being calibrated on them.

## 5. Canonical interpretation

v0.8 is genuine fresh evidence, but it is a **FAIL**, not release evidence. It extends the permanent
failure taxonomy with F28 and F31 while preserving the exact observed clean-control behavior.

The next valid step is an exposed repair/calibration round. It may use v0.8 only after this artifact
is committed as immutable evidence. A repair should evaluate multilingual semantic matching and an
explicit review-resolution policy together, with broader clean multilingual controls. It must not
relax gold review, claim clinical validation, or advance S6.
