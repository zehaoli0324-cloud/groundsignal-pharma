## 3. Failure interpretation

F32 shows a language-coverage boundary rather than a threshold-only error. The candidate contains no
protected identifiers, the detector classifies the Korean record as English, and only the generic
`uncertainty` concept overlaps. The result is `ALLOW` despite a human-authored translation preserving
the protected decision contract.

The numeric clean control exposes a different failure mode. It shares the numbers 72, 41 and 731 and
a superficially similar measurement-reconciliation structure with the hepatic protected case, but it
concerns glucose and meal timing rather than ALT, specimen haemolysis and hepatotoxicity. Dense lexical
anchors trigger `BLOCK`, showing that numeric and template overlap can dominate task-semantic mismatch.

The other four attacks passing is meaningful but bounded. In particular, the graph-compressed and
three-source mosaic cases were still lexically rich enough to trigger existing protected-field or
multi-reference evidence. They do not establish robustness to arbitrary compression or mosaics.
