## Scope

S5 v0.4 exposed four hard trust-root failures (F12–F15). This version repairs those
failure classes generically and reruns the already-exposed v0.4 suite as regression
evidence only. It does **not** create new fresh evidence and does not alter the v0.4
first observation.

## Repair

The protected training-export boundary now has an authority layer above caller data:

1. `medical/configs/s5-trust-policy-registry-v0.4.1.json` is the canonical registry.
   Its Git blob identity is pinned in `scripts/export_training_data.py`.
2. Only registry-listed policy paths or policy objects exactly matching a registered
   policy can authorize export. Arbitrary caller-built dictionaries and off-repository
   policy files fail closed.
3. The registered policy blob binds canonical suite path/blob, family root,
   manifest path/blob, source case path/blob, split, and variant metadata.
4. `scripts/s5_trust_policy.py` rejects duplicate `case_id` values across suites in
   one policy and rejects an ordinary-source blob that is byte-identical to a
   benchmark source-case blob.
5. The prior canonical v0.3.1 policy remains explicitly registered so exposed F8–F11
   regression can still be reproduced without making arbitrary policies trusted.
