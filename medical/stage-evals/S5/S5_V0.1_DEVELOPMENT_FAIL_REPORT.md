# S5 v0.1 Development Audit — First Observation FAIL

## Decision

**S5 release: FAIL / NOT RELEASED.**

This is a development audit of the already exposed P0 benchmark factory at snapshot `9e8aef6ee4e54dd6fe01eb6749368d2c15471762`. It is not fresh held-out evidence and must never be re-described as such.

## What passed

- 12/12 families satisfy the intended 5-case family composition.
- 60 case references are present: 36 development, 12 regression, 12 held-out-labelled P0 cases.
- 12/12 families declare held-out leakage policy metadata.
- 12/12 families declare at least four target capabilities and four failure hypotheses.
- Existing P0 materialization/referential-integrity validation passes.
- The model harness passed a sentinel leakage probe: gold-only material placed in `expected_behavior`, `graph_eval`, `safety`, and `scoring` was not rendered into the model-facing prompt.

These passes establish useful fixture hygiene. They do not override the hard failures below.

## Hard failures

### S5-F1 — SPLIT_PROVENANCE_NOT_ENFORCED

`clinical-case.schema.json` does not contain or require `family_id` or `split`. Split identity lives in the family manifest, while `export_training_data.py` consumes case/evaluation objects without resolving the family manifest. In the behavioral probe, an explicitly `split=heldout` synthetic case was exportable once its training candidate was marked approved.

This is a benchmark-contamination boundary defect. It is structural and must be fixed generically; no individual held-out case ID should be hardcoded.

### S5-F2 — GOLD_READINESS_UNSATISFIED

All 12 family manifests remain `gold_review_needed`; 0/12 are `gold_approved`. Therefore the P0 families remain development fixtures. This report does not invent expert review, clinical validation, or gold approval.

### S5-F3 — DECISION_CONTRACT_NOT_SCHEMA_ENFORCED

The case schema does not require `graph_eval`, and its graph contract does not require `required_node_ids`, `required_edge_ids`, or `expected_reasoning_path`. Schema-enforced decision-node components are 0/3. Current files may contain such fields, but S5 cannot rely on optional convention for future case materialization.

## Safety consequence

Until the above gates are repaired and re-evaluated:

- S5 must not be marked PASS;
- existing P0 `heldout` labels must not be treated as fresh or release-grade evidence;
- S6 may run against these cases for development, but downstream release logic must not automatically trust S5 split labels or gold status;
- training export must be treated as contamination-sensitive.

## Next version

The next rational version is **S5 v0.1.1** with two generic interface changes: immutable case-level family/suite/split provenance plus fail-closed training export, and a schema-enforced decision contract with typed exemption semantics. A release predicate should also preserve `gold_review_needed` as development-only without fabricating review.

After that repair, this v0.1 audit becomes exposed regression evidence. Only after the repaired implementation is frozen should a genuinely new S5 family/suite be constructed for first-use fresh held-out evaluation.
