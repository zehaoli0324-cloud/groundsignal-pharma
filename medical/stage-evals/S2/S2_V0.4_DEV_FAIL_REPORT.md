## 术语表

- **Negation-aware routing（否定感知路由）**：区分一个来源/意图词是用户真正要求的，还是处在“不查/不要/not/rather than”等排除作用域中。
- **Feature polarity（特征极性）**：每个命中特征被标记为正向 `POSITIVE` 或排除/否定 `NEGATIVE`，只有正向特征进入 intent scoring（意图评分）。
- **Source exclusion（来源排除）**：明确被用户排除的来源不会继续留在 ranked source list（来源排序列表）中。
- **Development/exposed（开发/已暴露）**：用于结构调试和防回归，不构成独立泛化证据。

## 4. Failure taxonomy — frozen before repair

No repair was performed after this FAIL.

### F1 — Coordinated exclusion scope does not propagate across a list

Observed in `S2N-001`: `不涉及监管、说明书或试验注册，我只要同行评议文献...`

The initial exclusion cue `不涉及` semantically scopes over a coordinated list, but v0.4 only inspects a short local prefix immediately before each feature. `试验注册` therefore remains positive and wins over the intended literature route.

General defect: exclusion scope is modeled as mention-local distance rather than a clause-level coordination span.

### F2 — Negation cue and feature head are separated by modifiers

Observed in `S2N-009`: `不要一般 study design guidance；我要查这个药是不是 CYP3A inhibitor。`

`不要` applies to the full noun phrase `一般 study design guidance`, but the current matcher expects the exclusion cue to be nearly adjacent to the individual feature occurrence. `study design` and `guidance` remain positive and incorrectly trigger `DDI_METHOD_GUIDANCE`.

General defect: exclusion should bind to a noun-phrase/clause span, not require immediate adjacency to each lexical feature.

### F3 — Context entity and excluded intent are incorrectly collapsed into one feature group

Observed in `S2N-011`: `我要中国国家临床路径，不查 CDE 受理或审评状态。`

`CDE` is correctly recognized inside an excluded regulatory clause. However `CDE` and the positive jurisdiction token `中国` belong to the same `china` feature group. The current “any negative hit suppresses the whole group” rule removes the positive jurisdiction context required by `CHINA_NATIONAL_CLINICAL_PATHWAY`, causing fallback to PubMed.

General defect: entity/jurisdiction context and task-intent evidence need separate polarity state; a negative mention of one alias must not erase an independent positive alias with a different semantic role.
